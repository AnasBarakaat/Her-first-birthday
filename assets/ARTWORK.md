# Artwork prompts

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool. The ocean image was compressed to WebP for the site. The character sheet preserves transparency and is drawn by quadrant.

## ocean.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Create a beautiful background image for a phone-first Ponyo-inspired underwater birthday game. Landscape 1536x1024 hand-painted watercolor gouache anime sea background, rich deep jade teal water, luminous pale aqua surface along very top, sunlight rays, delicate bubbles, gently undulating kelp and coral pink anemones along lower corners and bottom edge, tiny distant fish silhouettes. Dreamy cinematic Japanese animated film environment. Main central 70 percent is clean open teal water so game sprites and text can overlay. No people, no Ponyo, no typography or UI, no text, no border.

## characters.png

Use case: illustration-story. A game sprite sheet on genuinely transparent background, landscape layout 1536x1024. Four separate full body character sprites, exactly one in each quadrant with generous transparent space separating them. Top left: sleeping fish-form Ponyo, orange fluffy hair, round cream face, closed eyes, coral red round fish body with small cream fins, horizontally floating facing right. Top right: same fish-form Ponyo awake swimming facing right, matching the official Studio Ghibli movie design exactly: very large round eyes with big solid black pupils filling most of the eye and a single small white glint highlight, no visible iris ring, plain cream/peach face with absolutely no blush, no pink cheek circles, no cheek shading of any kind. Bottom left: adorable cream and ginger cat swimming horizontally facing right, wearing a teal scuba mask and tiny peach swim fins. Bottom right: adorable dark gray cat swimming facing right wearing pink scuba mask and tiny yellow swim fins. Hand painted Japanese animated movie watercolor gouache, clean silhouettes, charming and detailed. No background, no lettering, no grid lines, no extra characters. All four sprites fully contained inside their quadrants.
